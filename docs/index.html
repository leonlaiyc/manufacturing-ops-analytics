Fab Simulation, Monitoring & Decision Support

A fab simulation and decision engine built for AI agents to call

Fab operations decisions are hard for three reasons: local metrics contradict each other, equipment degrades quietly, and every option is expensive. This project connects process diagnosis of a real production log with a synthetic fab-style line whose bottleneck and anomalies are known by design, into one data-to-decision workflow: capacity investment, maintenance timing, dispatching policy, and yield trade-offs are tested in simulation, with a traceable record, before they become decisions. This is the simulation decision layer that agentic smart-manufacturing architectures call before an AI agent acts on the real line.

支援 AI Agent 呼叫的 fab 模擬決策引擎

半導體產線的營運決策難在三件事：局部指標互相矛盾、設備劣化悄悄發生、而每一個選項都很貴。本專案把真實生產紀錄的流程診斷，與一條瓶頸和異常皆為已知設計的合成產線，接成同一套從資料到決策的流程：產能投資、維修時機、派工策略與良率取捨，先在模擬中驗證、留下可追溯的紀錄，再成為決策。這正是 agentic 智慧製造架構中，AI agent 在對真實產線行動之前，用來評估選項的模擬決策層。

1

Process reconstruction

Process-mine a real production log to recover the actual paths of re-entrant and batch flows and locate where time is lost.

流程還原

用 process mining 從真實製程紀錄還原重入式與批次流程的實際路徑，定位時間損失。

2

Synthetic line model

A transparent, hand-built line with a batch furnace and re-entrant litho; the bottleneck and anomalies are known by design, so method quality is measured, not just demonstrated.

合成產線模型

手工打造含批次爐管、重入式微影的透明產線；瓶頸與異常皆為已知設計，方法的偵測與診斷品質因此可以被量測，而不是只被展示。

3

Decision support

Every operational option goes through what-if simulation comparing cycle time, yield risk, and cost, producing traceable decision evidence.

決策支援

把每一個營運選項放進 what-if 模擬，比較 cycle time、良率風險與成本的取捨，輸出可追溯的決策依據。

Three core highlights三大核心亮點

01

System-level counterfactuals against intuition-driven capacity mistakes

Local metrics often contradict each other: the furnace has the longest batch time and carries the most lots per run, while litho runs at the highest utilization. This project uses common-random-number (CRN) paired what-if simulation so every option is compared under identical random conditions. In the project's baseline scenario, adding the right tool improves mean cycle time 3.5 times more than the intuitive candidate; an investment-stress scenario with differentiated tool costs then shows how the decision returns to the business objective when operational and financial rankings reverse. These figures are rankings under stated assumptions, not real-fab benefit claims; what they demonstrate is that the workflow can evaluate capacity investments quantitatively.

系統級反事實分析，擋下直覺式的產能誤判

局部指標常常互相矛盾：爐管單批加工時間最長、一次可處理最多 lots，微影機台的稼動率卻最高。本專案用共同隨機數（CRN）配對的 what-if 模擬，讓每個方案在完全相同的隨機條件下對比。在本專案的基準情境下，加對一台機台對平均 cycle time 的改善，是直覺候選方案的 3.5 倍；再引入機台成本差異化的投資壓力情境，展示營運排名與財務排名反轉時，決策如何回到業務目標本身。這些數字是給定假設下的排序依據，不是實廠效益承諾；它們證明的是這套流程具備量化評估產能投資的能力。

When metrics conflict

If the priority is shortening cycle time, the better choice may be paying higher cost for a larger cycle-time improvement.

If short-term financial return is the priority, the better choice may be the option with the strongest net saving or fastest payback.

If both matter, first define the minimum cycle-time improvement required, then choose the most cost-effective option that meets that threshold.

當指標衝突時

如果優先目標是縮短 cycle-time，較好的選擇可能是支付較高成本換取較大的 cycle-time 改善。

如果優先目標是短期財務回報，較好的選擇可能是淨節省最高或回收最快的方案。

如果兩者都重要，先定義最低需要的 cycle-time 改善門檻，再選擇符合門檻且最具成本效益的方案。

Operational impact, investment cost, and business objective must be evaluated together to find the best answer for the current situation.

營運影響、投資成本與商業目標必須綜合評估，在不同的當下情境找出最佳解。

02

Slow-drift monitoring: turning detection time into decision value

When equipment degrades slowly, output metrics often show nothing until congestion cost has already accumulated. The monitoring design moves the question from "was it detected" to "what is the detection time worth": EWMA, a transparent, classic control-chart method sensitive to small persistent drift, serves as the baseline; labeled anomaly scenarios measure its detection delay and false-alarm rate; and the alert time is converted into the cost still avoidable. In a 160-day synthetic backtest, an output-only monitor stays silent over the whole horizon, while EWMA alerts 54 days after degradation onset, when most (about 95%) of the extra congestion cost is still avoidable under illustrative waiting-cost assumptions. This is not a real-fab savings promise; it shows how the workflow turns detection time into decision value. The same monitoring design lets equipment-health risk scores and forecast residuals feed the same alerting and costing pipeline, matching the industry's move from univariate control charts toward predictive monitoring.

慢性劣化監控：把偵測時間轉成決策價值

設備緩慢劣化時，產出指標往往毫無警訊，等產出真的掉下來，壅塞成本早已累積。本專案的監控設計把問題從「有沒有偵測到」推進到「偵測到的時間值多少」：以 EWMA 這個透明、經典、對小幅持續漂移敏感的管制圖方法作為基線，用標註好的異常情境量測偵測延遲與誤報率，再把警報時間點換算成仍可避免的成本。在 160 天的合成回測中，只看產出的監控整段期間沒有發出任何警報；EWMA 在劣化開始後 54 天發出警報，在示意等待成本假設下，此時大部分（約 95%）的額外壅塞成本仍可避免。這不是實廠節省承諾，而是展示這套流程如何把偵測時間轉成決策價值。同一套監控架構的設計，讓設備健康模型的風險分數與預測殘差可以接入同一條警報與成本評估流程，對應業界從單變數管制圖走向預測式監控的演進。

Where does $249k come from? Waiting is priced at an illustrative $10 per lot-hour in queue, and the gap is measured against a no-degradation twin of the same run. Degradation makes queues grow on themselves, so the cost accelerates: the first 54 days accumulate only ≈$12k, the next 76 days ≈$237k. The rate is an assumption; the structure is not: cost accelerates, and early detection saves most of it.

$249k 是怎麼算出來的？等待以示意費率 $10／lot·小時計價，成本差距是與同一次 run 的無劣化對照（twin）相比。劣化會讓排隊自我放大，所以成本是 加速累積的：前 54 天只累積約 $12k，後 76 天累積約 $237k。費率是假設；但結構不是假設： 成本會加速累積，及早偵測能省下大部分。

另一個優勢是這個流程可以 backtest：先檢查 drift detector 是否能在 output 明顯變差前找到問題，再估算及早行動可以避免多少後續成本。

03

One engine, four classes of fab operations questions

Is the capacity investment worth it; maintain now or wait; which dispatching rule fits current demand and quality targets; does faster flow buy queue-time violations and rework risk: these four question classes share one simulation engine and one evidence standard, and every answer traces back to a logged simulation run. An AI agent can call the engine to run what-ifs and assemble the results into a decision memo in which every number is checkable, in line with the agentic smart-manufacturing direction Samsung showed at GTC 2026. The re-entrant and batch structures modeled here are methodologically isomorphic to advanced-packaging capacity problems (HBM stacking, bonding/molding batch tools): the analysis workflow proven here transfers to packaging bottleneck scenarios. That is method transferability, not a claim of completed modeling.

一顆引擎，回答四類 fab 營運問題

產能投資值不值得、維修現在做還是等一等、哪個派工規則在當前需求與品質目標下較好、更快的流動會不會換來 queue time 超限與重工風險：這四類問題共用同一顆模擬引擎與同一套證據標準，每個答案都能追溯到一筆模擬紀錄。AI agent 可以直接呼叫這顆引擎執行 what-if，把結果整理成每個數字都可查證的決策備忘錄，對齊 Samsung 於 GTC 2026 展示的 agentic 智慧製造方向。專案建模的重入式與批次結構，與先進封裝（HBM 堆疊、bonding/molding 批次製程）的產能問題在方法上同構：這裡驗證的分析流程可以遷移到封裝瓶頸情境，這是方法的可轉移性，不是已完成的建模宣稱。

Methodology and validation方法與驗證

Ground truth by design: the synthetic line's bottleneck and anomalies are injected on purpose, so detection and diagnosis quality is measured against a known answer; synthetic data is labeled synthetic throughout.

Consistency check: the simulation core passes a Little's Law internal-consistency check with a long-run WIP gap of 0.2%; per simulation V&V methodology this is a check of internal model correctness, not a validity claim about any real fab.

Scope boundary: the cost model produces only relative rankings under stated assumptions with sensitivity checks; the real production log is used only for defensible process reconstruction and diagnosis; a live data link to a physical fab (the defining feature of a digital twin) is explicitly outside the boundary.

References: Little (1961, Operations Research); Sargent (2013, Journal of Simulation); NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 6.3.2.4; Capezza et al. (2024, Journal of Quality Technology).

已知真值設計：合成產線的瓶頸與異常皆為刻意注入，方法的偵測與診斷品質有標準答案可對照量測；合成資料全程明示為合成。

一致性檢查：模擬核心以 Little's Law 做內部一致性檢查，長期運行的 WIP 誤差 0.2%；依模擬驗證方法學，此為模型內部正確性檢查，不是對任何真實工廠的有效性宣稱。

適用邊界：成本模型只做給定假設下的相對排序與敏感度分析；真實生產紀錄僅用於可辯護的流程還原與診斷；與實體工廠的即時資料連線（數位孿生的定義性特徵）明確劃在邊界之外。

參考文獻： Little (1961, Operations Research)、 Sargent (2013, Journal of Simulation)、 NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook 6.3.2.4、 Capezza et al. (2024, Journal of Quality Technology)。
