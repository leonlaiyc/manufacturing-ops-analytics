01

Local metrics show candidates, not the final answer

Local metrics identify which stations deserve attention, but they do not directly answer which capacity addition improves the whole line most. FURNACE is slow per batch and processes the most lots per run, while LITHO has the highest utilization. The decision needs a system-level what-if comparison.

局部指標能指出候選站點，但不是最後答案

局部指標可以告訴我們哪些站點值得注意，但不能直接回答「加哪一站的產能，對整條線改善最大」。 FURNACE 單批加工時間最長、每次運轉可處理最多 lots；LITHO 的稼動率最高。因此還需要用整體系統的 what-if 比較來判斷。

Under station-specific cost assumptions, the option with the largest cycle-time reduction may not be the easiest financial choice.

當 cycle-time 改善與投資成本拉向不同方向

Calculation: Net cost change = scenario total cost - baseline total cost. Total cost includes processing cost (tool busy-hours × illustrative processing rate), waiting / congestion cost (lot queue-wait hours × illustrative holding rate), and added tool cost (number of added tools × illustrative tool cost). Negative saves versus baseline; positive costs more.

計算方式：淨成本變化 = 方案總成本 − 基準總成本

總成本包含：

  • 處理成本（工具忙碌時數 × 示意處理費率）
  • 等待 / 壅塞成本（lot 排隊等待時數 × 示意持有費率）

（負值代表相對基準省錢；正值代表成本增加）

Processing consumes tool time, energy, labor, and maintenance resources. Waiting creates WIP burden, longer lead time, and congestion risk.

處理會消耗工具時間、能源、人力與維護資源；等待會造成 WIP 負擔、lead time 變長與壅塞風險。

Break-even added-tool cost asks: how expensive can the added tool be before the option stops being net-saving? In this run the thresholds are LITHO $32.0k, FURNACE $9.7k, DEPO $3.3k, and METRO $0.6k.

新增工具的損益平衡成本回答的是：新增工具可以多貴，才不會讓方案從淨節省變成淨增加？本次結果為 LITHO $32.0k、FURNACE $9.7k、DEPO $3.3k、METRO $0.6k。

When metrics conflict

If lead time or WIP reduction is the priority, paying for the larger LITHO improvement may be justified.

If short-term financial return is the priority, the better choice may be the option with the strongest net saving or fastest payback.

If both matter, first define the minimum cycle-time improvement required, then choose the most cost-effective option that meets that threshold.

當指標衝突時

如果優先目標是縮短 cycle-time，較好的選擇可能是支付較高成本換取較大的 cycle-time 改善。

如果優先目標是短期財務回報，較好的選擇可能是淨節省最高或回收最快的方案。

如果兩者都重要，先定義最低需要的 cycle-time 改善門檻，再選擇符合門檻且最具成本效益的方案。

The point is not that one metric automatically wins. The point is to make the trade-off explicit: operational impact, investment cost, and business objective must be evaluated together. Under a lead-time-first objective, LITHO may still be the right choice. Under a cost-first objective, the recommendation may change.

營運影響、投資成本與商業目標必須綜合評估，在不同的當下情境找出最佳解。

03

Slow degradation may not cut output first, but it raises cost early

Equipment degradation does not always reduce output immediately. Processing time can drift upward first, causing queue wait and WIP cost to accumulate while output still looks normal.

緩慢劣化不一定先讓 output 下降，但會先推高成本

設備劣化不一定會立刻讓 output 明顯下降。處理時間可能先慢慢上升，讓 queue wait 和 WIP 成本開始累積，而 output 看起來仍然正常。

01

Monitor signal

Track daily median lot cycle time, not just output.

監控訊號

追蹤每日 lot cycle time 中位數，不只看 output。

02

Fit clean baseline

Fit the baseline only on clean pre-anomaly days, so future anomaly data cannot tune the threshold.

建立乾淨 baseline

只用已知乾淨的異常前資料建立 baseline，避免用到未來異常資料調整門檻。

03

Detect slow drift

Use EWMA to accumulate small persistent deviations that a classic control chart may miss.

偵測緩慢漂移

用 EWMA 累積小而持續的偏移，補足傳統 control chart 可能漏掉的慢性劣化。

Calculation: compare cumulative cost between the degraded line and its clean twin. Total degradation cost is the cost gap at the end of the run. Cost already incurred is the cost gap at the alert day. Avoidable cost is the remaining gap after the alert, assuming action is taken after detection.

計算方式：比較 degraded line 與 clean twin 的累積成本差。總劣化成本是模擬結束時的成本差。已發生成本是 alert day 當下的成本差。可避免成本是在 alert 之後仍未發生的剩餘差額，前提是假設偵測後會採取行動。

This is a backtested monitoring setup, not a guarantee that every real degradation will be caught on day 84. The point is to validate the detector against a known degradation start and translate alert timing into avoidable cost.

這是 backtested monitoring setup，不保證每一次真實劣化都會在 day 84 被抓到。重點是用已知劣化起始日驗證 detector，並把 alert 時間轉換成可避免成本。
