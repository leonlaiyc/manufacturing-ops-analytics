Production data tells you what happened. This project turns it into what to do: which station is the constraint (and the proof), when a KPI drifts out of control, and what a capacity or demand change would do to cycle time, throughput, and cost.

Synthetic line data is clearly labeled synthetic — it exists to give the methods a known ground truth (engineered bottleneck, labeled anomalies) so they can be validated, not just demonstrated. The real production log is used for diagnosis only. Cost rates are illustrative; conclusions are rankings tested under ±50% sensitivity.
